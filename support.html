Support (English)

Purchase missing? Settings → “All of Olympus” → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.
Phone gets warm? Choose Graphics: Balanced or Low and keep Battery saver on.
Progress lost? Progress is stored on the device only; deleting the app deletes it.
